# B5.1: lanes=8 heap-use-after-free in janet_chanat_mark — fix plan

## Root cause (fresh ASAN captures, this session)
- Repro `bench/agents-lanes.janet 5000 4 8`, 5/6 crash under the working ASAN recipe.
- Capture A (matches handoff): UAF read in `janet_chanat_mark` items loop; freed 32-byte region, freed by T0.
- Capture B (NEW, decisive): UAF read at ev.c:1328 (`data[i]`) on the channel's `items.data` array itself — freed **by realloc on T6** (a lane-worker push growing the queue) while T7's GC iterated it unlocked. **Revises the handoff's T0-only lead**: the freeing thread is whichever thread mutates the channel under the lock; T0 just does the most pushes/frees (lane0 dispatch + teardown).
- All queue mutation (push growth realloc, pop+unpack freeing transit buffers, pending claims, deinit) happens under the channel lock. `janet_chanat_mark` is the only unlocked reader → races.
- Why lock-the-mark failed before (attempt 1): marking while holding the channel lock recurses into marking other threaded abstracts → lock-ordering livelock at N=20000. Why snapshot failed (attempt 2): still touched racy memory / own malloc on a corrupted heap.

## Key insight making a clean fix possible
For THREADED channels, marking the internals is unnecessary, not just racy:
- `items` only holds immediates or transit buffers from `janet_chan_pack` — raw `janet_malloc` memory, not GC objects on any heap; lifetime is queue ownership (freed only by popper/deinit under lock).
- Every fiber parked on a threaded channel is `janet_gcroot`ed by its own VM at enqueue (ev.c push ~1629 and pop ~1748 — the only two enqueue sites, both root when `is_threaded`).
Plain/isolated channels are single-VM by contract; their pendings are unrooted and items heap-shared → keep full mark.

## Fix (narrowest)
`janet_chanat_mark`: early-return for `janet_chan_is_threaded(chan)`; unchanged behavior for plain/isolated. No lock, no snapshot, no malloc.
